# MORROWMERE privacy policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

MORROWMERE is an offline single-player game. The application does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or device data. It contains no advertising, analytics, account system, social features, or third-party tracking SDK.

Game progress, settings, and the optional player-entered character name are stored locally on the user's device. This information is used only to resume the game. Removing the application's storage or uninstalling the application deletes that local data, subject to the device platform's backup behavior.

The application does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, advertising identifiers, or other sensitive device capabilities.

This policy may change if a future release adds online services. Any such release will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration before distribution.
