# Morrowmere Audio Sources

Most Chronicle I audio is project-owned procedural synthesis. The remastered
opening score and selected physical combat cues use the following CC0 1.0
source recordings. CC0 does not require attribution, but the sources are
retained here for transparent release provenance.

## Opening score

- **Fantasy Orchestral Theme** — Joth — CC0 1.0 —
  <https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-orchestral-theme>

The opening uses one continuous passage so its melody and orchestration remain
coherent beneath the narration. The selected passage begins with restrained
travel tension, rises at the first-arrow turn, and fades beneath the title.

## Combat and cinematic effects

- **Battle Sound Effects** — artisticdude — CC0 1.0 —
  <https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects>
- **20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)** — StarNinjas — CC0 1.0 —
  <https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes>

The shipped files are mono MP3 remasters with scene-specific filtering,
loudness control, and mobile-safe peaks. Original source URLs and hashes are
also recorded in `production/chronicle1/media/audio-provenance.json`.
